# Media and branding
The Royal Table and Shuvi 360 are demo brand labels, not claims of a real restaurant. Public site uses illustrative Unsplash photography from the URLs maintained in src/lib/demo-menu.ts; source credits are available on /credits. See https://unsplash.com/license for license conditions. These photos do not depict an actual Royal Table business. Individual photographer/source verification and release review remain required before a real client launch.
Most sample catalog dishes use category/icon placeholders. Do not misrepresent them as real restaurant photographs. Replace with client-owned or appropriately licensed media before production. Lucide icons, generated brand SVG/PNG and QR output are UI assets. No customer or private restaurant images are included.
